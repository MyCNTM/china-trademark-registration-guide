## Class, similar group and item are different

| Level | Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Class | Broad Nice Classification category | Class 25: clothing, footwear and headwear |
| Similar group | China's grouping used in similarity assessment | 2501: clothing |
| Item | Specific goods/services wording in the application | 上衣 — tops [clothing] |

Your specification should describe the goods or services you actually provide or genuinely intend to provide. The Chinese wording matters for the filing; the English translation helps you understand and review it.

## Similar groups help planning; they do not guarantee coverage

Do not assume that selecting one item protects every item in its group, or that items in different groups can never be similar. Group notes, cross-references and the facts of the case can affect the analysis. Simply spreading ten unrelated items across ten groups is not a sound substitute for a business-based specification.

For a clothing brand, identify the actual products first: garments, footwear, headwear and other accessories can lead to different groups or classes. Then check acceptable wording and relevant earlier marks for those goods.

## Does every online shop need Class 35?

No. Selling your own goods does not automatically mean you provide a Class 35 service to others. Assess the actual activity, such as advertising or business services provided for other businesses, and select the relevant goods classes as well. See [CNIPA's Class 35 guidance](https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/attach/0/762552d25fb743458cbc048a71a8c083.pdf).

## Use the tool to prepare a working list

1. Search in English or Chinese and inspect the results in context.
2. Add appropriate items and review the class and group for each.
3. Export or copy the list for discussion with the service team.
4. Confirm the final Chinese wording and written quote before filing.

Our standard package includes up to ten items per class for one mark at USD 199; extra items cost USD 10 each. The tool's estimate excludes separately quoted proceedings.
