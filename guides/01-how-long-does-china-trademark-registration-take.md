# How long does China trademark registration take?

*Reviewed 18 November 2026.*

For a straightforward direct application, we currently use **around 9–12 months** as a planning estimate. This is not a guaranteed completion date: examination queries, refusal, opposition and procedural delays can extend the process.

## The stages to plan for

| Stage | What happens | What you can prepare |
|---|---|---|
| Before filing | Search, goods selection and document checks | Final mark, applicant identity and actual goods/services |
| Filing | Application is submitted and formalities are checked | Correct applicant details and signed authority |
| Examination | CNIPA considers registrability and earlier rights | A response if an objection arises |
| Publication | An approved application is published for opposition | Monitor the applicable deadline |
| Registration | Registration follows if the remaining requirements are satisfied | Check certificate details and retain records |

An application receipt confirms filing; it does not mean registration has been granted or that use is free from conflict.

## Statutory periods are not a total turnaround promise

Article 28 of the [2019 Trademark Law](https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2019/7/30/art_95_28179.html) provides a nine-month examination period. Article 33 provides a three-month opposition period. These are separate procedural rules, not a promise that every application will finish within a fixed total number of months.

The [2026 revision](https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2026/6/26/art_95_206942.html) takes effect on **1 January 2027** and provides a two-month opposition period in Article 36. Check the law and any applicable transition arrangements for the particular publication; do not assume that every pending case switches automatically to the new period.

## What usually creates extra work?

- An unclear goods description or incorrect applicant information.
- An earlier mark that leads to refusal or a need to narrow the filing.
- An opposition during publication.
- A review, appeal or separate action against an earlier registration.

Those procedures have their own deadlines and are outside a straightforward registration estimate. We will quote and explain them separately if needed.

## Plan before production or launch

Search before committing to packaging, labels or a factory order. Filing early matters, but a filing alone does not resolve an earlier-right conflict. Keep dated evidence of your brand development and commercial use, and allow time to consider alternative names.
