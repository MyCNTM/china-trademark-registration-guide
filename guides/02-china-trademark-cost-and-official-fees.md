# China trademark registration costs and fees

*Reviewed 18 November 2026.*

Our standard direct-filing package is **USD 199 per trademark, per class**, including the official filing fee and our service fee for up to ten goods or services items in that class. Each additional item in the same class costs **USD 10**.

This is MY CNTM's combined service price, not the amount of the government fee alone.

## Worked examples

| Filing | Calculation | Package total |
|---|---|---:|
| One mark, one class, 10 items | 1 × USD 199 | USD 199 |
| One mark, one class, 14 items | USD 199 + 4 × USD 10 | USD 239 |
| One mark, three classes, up to 10 items each | 3 × USD 199 | USD 597 |
| Two marks, three classes each, up to 10 items each | 6 × USD 199 | USD 1,194 |

The ten-item allowance applies separately to each class. Unused places in one class do not offset extra items in another. Separate word and logo applications are separate filings.

## What affects the final quote?

Confirm the mark, applicant and Chinese goods specification before paying. A broad English description may need to be replaced by several acceptable Chinese items. The classification tool estimates the package for one mark; the written quote confirms the actual scope.

Refusal responses, reviews, oppositions, invalidations, corrections and other additional proceedings are quoted separately. Special document or priority requirements should also be confirmed for the particular case. A filing fee does not buy a guaranteed registration.

## Payment and next steps

We first review the search result and proposed specification with you. After approval of the written quote, use the payment options and instructions in your portal order. Bank or intermediary charges may depend on the payment route; follow the current invoice instructions.

## Comparing a Madrid designation

Madrid costs depend on the international application, classes, designated territories and applicable fees. Use the current [WIPO fee resources](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/madrid-system/how_to/file/fees) instead of a fixed historic CHF figure. Include potential local refusal-response costs when comparing routes.
