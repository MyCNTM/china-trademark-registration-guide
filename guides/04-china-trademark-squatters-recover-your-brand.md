# What if someone has already filed your brand in China?

*Reviewed 18 November 2026.*

An earlier application needs investigation. It is not automatically a bad-faith filing merely because you used the name abroad first. Start with the mark, applicant, dates, status and exact goods, then assess the available evidence and deadlines.

## Preserve evidence before deciding on a response

Keep dated brand materials, invoices, correspondence with manufacturers or distributors, evidence of reputation and any relationship with the earlier applicant. Preserve the original documents and their dates. A search listing alone does not prove that a mark has or has not been used.

## Different statuses require different options

| Situation | Possible route to assess | Important limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Application published for opposition | Opposition | The publication and applicable law determine the deadline |
| Mark already registered | Invalidation on an applicable ground | Grounds, standing, evidence and time limits vary |
| Registration potentially unused for three consecutive years | Non-use cancellation | Registration age alone does not establish non-use |
| Commercial resolution is realistic | Negotiated transfer | Verify ownership, terms and transfer formalities |
| Your own application has been refused | Refusal review or other response | Do not miss its independent response deadline |

Article 49 of the [2019 Trademark Law](https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2019/7/30/art_95_28179.html) addresses cancellation for three consecutive years of non-use without proper reason. A cancellation action does not transfer the mark to you, and success is not guaranteed.

For opposition periods around the 2027 law change, consult the [timeline guide](01-how-long-does-china-trademark-registration-take.md) and verify the arrangements applicable to the individual case.

## Keep your commercial plan separate from the dispute

Consider whether to file an alternative name, narrow the relevant goods or pause branded production while the risk is assessed. Registration of a new application does not by itself settle every earlier-right dispute. Export-only manufacture should not be assumed to avoid Chinese trademark risk.
